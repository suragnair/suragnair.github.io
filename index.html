I am a Principal ML Scientist at Genentech, working on machine learning for genomics and drug discovery, as well as evaluating and applying agentic AI systems. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Stanford University, where I worked on single-cell epigenomics and deep learning models for chromatin accessibility in the lab of Anshul Kundaje.

Previously, I interned at Apple on the Siri team. I also maintain open-source implementations of ML systems including AlphaZero and SeqGAN. I received my Bachelors in Electrical Engineering from IIT Delhi.
